Why these numbers

The assumptions, stated honestly
ConstantValueWhy
Ice at 0°C334 kJ/kg to meltThe latent heat of fusion - the melt does most of the chilling work
Meltwater warming4.186 kJ/kg/°CAfter melting, the ice-water keeps absorbing heat up to your target
Body displacement~70-80 LYou displace roughly your own mass in water - the tub needs less than you think
Working range10-15°CWhere most cold-exposure protocols actually live

Ambient heat gain during the chill is not modeled - add about 10% more ice on a hot day. And never plunge alone.
